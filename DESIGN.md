# Progression design

Bloxy aims at the feel of big modded packs: several routes of progress, each worth following for its own rewards, which feed into each other and give players more than one way to reach most goals.

## Rules

1. **Every objective has two or three solutions, one per route, each with its own flavour and tradeoffs**, and tiers within each so it improves gradually. A player who sticks to one route can still finish everything their own way.
2. **Routes feed each other only as ingredients.** A product of another route can be a part, catalyst or upgrade in your process (usually consumed once, when building something). Nothing converts one route's core resource into another's: lifeblood never becomes mana, essences never become power. Each route's core resource is always made by that route's own production.
3. **Dabbling is rewarded, not required.** Recipes that use another route's ingredient are cheaper or better versions of a route's own recipe, never the only way.
4. **Each route is interesting in itself**: it has rewards nothing else gives.

## Routes

| Route | Core resource | Made by | Rewards only it gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Industry | power (W, J), refined liquids | generators, pumps, refinery | automation: pipes, belts, crusher, auto-smelting, lamps |
| Botany | mana | Sunsip, Dewcup, Emberbell into cisterns | infusion, charms on gear, pocket rifts |
| Lifeblood | lifeblood | Ritual Knife, creatures slain by the altar | tablets, heartstones, sigils |
| Alchemy | essences | melting items in a heated Steeping Cauldron | transmutation (lumen, saplings, coal, feathers) |
| Delving | loot | dungeons, bosses | found gear, lumen, bone dust, the skill tree's combat side |

Platinum ore itself is rare: deep (below y 32), and only under peak and frost islands. Smelting it gives one platinum; every route gets more from it or makes it from gold.

## Carry more: how the two differ

- **Backpacks** (Industry): physical items with their own contents (`Gear::pack` names the hoard). You can carry several, hand one to a friend, or store a full one in a chest. Upgrading keeps the contents.
- **Pocket rifts** (Botany): one pocket per player, opened from any rift you carry, sized by your best rift. It can't be given away or stored, and nobody else can open it.

## Big goals (later)

Each needs two or three routes, whichever the player likes: your own waystone, an airship, a personal sanctum (claimed land that turns safe, keeps growing and grants powers). A Codex unlocks a page the first time you touch each route's key items.
