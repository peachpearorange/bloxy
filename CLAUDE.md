# Bloxy
A browser-first multiplayer voxel sandbox in Bevy 0.19.1, aiming at the feel of big modded block-building packs: a procedural ocean of diverse islands with ores, caves and trees, waystones to travel between them, mining and building, other players around you, and (later) machines, power and automation. Everything is procedural: no mesh or texture assets.

# Roles
`protocol::Role` (from opts): `Solo` (no network, local authority), `Host` (native window + WebSocket server), `Dedicated` (headless server, `MinimalPlugins`), `Guest` (connects to a server; the browser build is usually this or `Solo`). `authority` systems run for every role but `Guest`; `plays` systems for every role but `Dedicated`. Use these, not replicon's states, to gate game logic: replicon's `ClientState::Disconnected` also covers a guest that lost its connection.

Progression follows `DESIGN.md`: parallel routes (industry, botany, lifeblood, alchemy, delving), several ways to every objective, routes feeding each other only as ingredients, never by converting one route's core resource into another's.

# Modules
- `block.rs` — `Block` (u16 repr; `voxels::pack` writes ids below 255 as one byte and the rest as 255 plus a byte, so old saves still read; append new variants so saves and the wire keep their meaning; `look` → `Opaque/Cutout/Liquid/Invisible/Model/Log`, `leafy`, `modelled` (flowers, saplings, small mushrooms: `Poppy` to `BrownMushroom`, `ALL_MODELS`, torches, ladders and beds; not solid but targetable, plants break at once), `LADDERS` (`Ladder` hangs on the -Z wall, `LadderEast/South/West` the other walls) and `WALL_TORCHES` (`WallTorch…`: a `Torch` placed on a block's side, leaning out like Minecraft's; `torch` is any of them; they drop when their wall is dug) (`wall`, `against(wall)` picks the variant when placing, `held` maps every variant back to the `Ladder`, `Torch` or `Bed` item), `BEDS` (2×1 beds: a foot and a head half for each direction the head lies; `laid(toward)`, `partner` points at the other half, `pillow` marks heads), `liquid` → `(Fluid, level)` (`Fluid::Water`/`Lava` sources are level 0, `WaterFlow1..7` / `LavaFlow1..3` the flowing levels; `Fluid::flowing(level)`, `reach`, `delay`), `fluid` (swum in, not solid), `waystone` (`Waystone` base and `WaystoneTop`, solid pedestal models), `station` (`CraftingTable`, `Furnace`), `sign` (`SIGNS`: each log with its sign: Oak, Birch, Spruce, Palm; modelled, drawn by `sign.rs` instead of the chunk mesh), `plant` (the jittered models), `item`, `tool` (`Boat`, `Balloon`, `Bucket`, `WaterBucket`, `LavaBucket`, `Feather`, `FishingRod`, `Charcoal`, the spell ingredients `ArcaneDust`, `BoneDust`, `LizardClaw`, `Candle`, the `FISH` (Cod, Salmon, TropicalFish, Pufferfish), the `SPELLS` (`spell`: one consumable item per spell, crafted once its talent is learned, used up by each cast), the `TOOLS` (`forged`: Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Sword, their material in the stack's `gear::Gear`) and the wearables (`worn`: Hat, Amulet, Mail, Belt, Boots, Ring): items only, not `placeable`), dungeon blocks (`AncientBrick`, `MossyBrick`, `CrackedBrick`, `CarvedStone` with glowing runes, glowing `Lumen` crystal, `BonePile`, `Cobweb` (cutout, not solid, slows whoever is inside), `furnished` solid models `Brazier` (a light like the torch: `lit`), `Urn` (breaks into a random fuel), `Chest` (unbreakable while it holds anything; right-click opens it, `chest.rs`), `EmptyChest` (breakable)), machines (`machine`: `CopperPipe` to `ElectricLampLit`, solid models with cube collisions, also `furnished`; `PIPES` in four tiers, `pipe`; `ElectricLampLit` is the lamp while powered, `held` maps it back), the unbreakable generated `OilSeep`, `stack` (most 64, buckets and spells 16, filled buckets, boats, rods, beds, tools and wearables 1), `carrying` (a filled bucket's `Fluid`; `Fluid::bucket`, `source`), `solid`, `softness` (how rounded `mesh.rs` draws it: soils and leaves 1, stone 0.6, cobbles 0.4, built blocks 0), `breakable` (not bedrock or waystones), `ROCKS` (each rock with the cobble it drops: Greywacke, Granite, Diorite, Andesite, Limestone, Slate; there is no generic "stone" block: the grey rock is `Greywacke`, its cobble `GreywackeCobble`, cut into `GreywackeBricks`; serde aliases read the old `Stone`, `Cobblestone`, `StoneBricks` names), `rock`, `seconds_to_break`, `drop`, `tiles` = top/side/bottom `Tile`s). Add a block: variant + every match, and a `Tile` with its `paint`. `TallGrass` is retired: never generated, not an item, read back as air from saved columns (`voxels::unpack`); its variant only keeps the ids after it.
- Sea life and the deep (`block.rs`): `organism` (`Kelp` to `Clam`: kelp, seagrass, brain coral and sponge (solid, `Surface` textured), fan and tube coral, anemone, glow polyp, vent worms, giant clam), `waterlogged` organisms are models that count as water (`wet`, `sunk`: swimming, the underwater camera, sea creatures, mesh water faces, flow never washes them away; digging one leaves `Water`); `Abyssite`, `VentStone`, `NoduleOre` on the abyssal floor; precursor `PrecursorAlloy`, `PrecursorGlass`, `AetherConduit`, `AetherLamp`; items `Pearl`, `AetherCell`, `AlloyScrap`, `ResonantCrystal`, `Datacore`, `JellyGel` (lanternjellies), `Torpedo`; the `BioLab` station and its products `Reefstone`, `Nacre`, `Biolume`, `Glowkelp`; the `Submarine` and its `module`s (`SonarModule` to `PowerCell`). `Surface` (Alloy, Conduit, Abyssal, Vent, Reef, Nacre, Brain, Sponge, Biolume, then the rocks Greywacke to Slate, Gravel, the ores CoalOre to PlatinumOre, Dirt, Sand, Clay, Basalt, Bedrock, the six cobbles, the turf blocks Grass, Snow and Mycelium, the four leaves, then the built surfaces `AncientBrick`, `MossyBrick`, `CrackedBrick`, `GreywackeBricks`, `CarvedStone`, `Planks` and the barks `OakBark` to `PalmBark` of the four logs; `joins` groups alloy with conduits) marks blocks drawn procedurally by `ground.wgsl` instead of the atlas (icons, held and loose items still use the atlas tiles).
- Connected procedural surfaces (`mesh.rs` `surfaced` + `ground.wgsl` `finish`): a `Surface` block's faces carry `-(SURFACED + kind * 256 + mask)` in `UV_1.y`, the mask holding which of the 8 in-plane neighbours (`tangents` of the face normal) continue the same surface (same `joins` group and also exposed on that side). The shader patterns everything from world position, so a contiguous run of blocks reads as one piece: `border` is the distance to the run's outline (inner corners too), used for bevelled frames and grooves on alloy (big panels seamed on a 3-block world grid, brushed metal, pulsing `traces` circuits in aether, denser on conduits), weathered rims and lichen-lit cellular cracks on abyssite, glowing fissures and sulphur crust on vent stone, cellular pits and algae on reefstone, view-dependent thin-film iridescence on nacre, labyrinthine ridges on brain coral, pores on sponge, breathing veins on biolume; `bumped` perturbs the normal from the height with screen-space derivatives (works in plain mode). Rocks, gravel, ores, soils and cobbles are `rocky`: smooth patterns made in the shader at the continuous world position (16 texels per block as the pattern scale; gradient noise (`supple`) for specks and clouds through the same palettes as the atlas, `ramped` along a quadratic B-spline of the four shades, its input eased softly into range instead of clamped, so gradients have no kinks or plateaus: greywacke as continuous layered grain with small soft flecks (no threshold blotches, which read as banding), faint warm/cool hue drift, rusty weathering stains, fracture lines from `cellular` (at least a pixel wide) between slightly differently toned facets, and a `relief` height (grain, facets, cracks; faded with distance) that `bumped` turns into lighting, and every bedrock rock (the six rocks, ores, basalt, bedrock) shares the fracture lines, facet tones, hue drift, stains and `relief`, each adding its own: granite and diorite as a fine crystal mosaic (`pebbles` at 2.6 per texel: pink feldspar, grey quartz, black biotite; diorite salt-and-pepper) fading to an average tone, andesite grain, limestone bedding and small solution pits, slate cleavage grooves, basalt joints, dirt (warped `pebbles` clods lit from the top left, faint broken crevices, dark crumbs, light flecks, a few small stones), rippled sand, banded clay, basalt columns (`pebbles` in the horizontal plane, joints and cross cracks), cracked bedrock, and gravel and cobbles as lit stones from `pebbles` (the nearest of the eight jittered cells around, so stones are never cut by cell lines; touching stones meet at a grit seam) in dark grit, ore blobs over stone in the ore's three shades, gold, diamond and platinum glowing; the dirt showing on grass, snow and mycelium sides is `rocky` dirt too), close up every rocky surface gains micro detail (two fine grain octaves at about 85 and 220 per block and small grit pits, switched on only once a texel covers several pixels, with bump heights scaled down with their size so the lighting stays smooth, and stronger where a broad `coarse` noise says so), and far away it fades to the palette's middle (keeping block-scale variation) once a texel is smaller than a pixel (`size`: texels per pixel from the geometric mean of the screen derivatives, so grazing views don't blur early), so it doesn't shimmer; thresholds are soft (`below`) so spots and seams have no hard outlines. There is no pixelated mode. Built surfaces (`crafted`, from `MASONRY` on): `masonry` lays staggered courses of per-brick tone with chipped mortar joints (ancient: 16/16-block bricks, greywacke: 8), moss creeping over tops and joints on mossy bricks, Voronoi cracks on cracked ones; `carved` is a brick frame round a sunken panel with three glowing procedural `rune`s (strokes on a 3x3 lattice from a per-face hash, a stem always) that pulse; `planked` lays 4-texel boards with staggered butt joints, grain, streaks, knots, a shadow gap and nails; `barked` shades log sides from 3D noise on the cylinder so it wraps seamlessly: oak furrows, birch lenticels and scars, spruce plates, palm ring scars, plus near-camera micro detail (vertical fibres, specks, flakes) in colour and relief; log ends get wobbly growth rings, a pith, a radial check and a bark rind (logs pass the code through `Builder::strip`/`polygon` in the cutout mesh). Turf is `turfed`: `rocky` dirt with its cover (grass: `fuzz`, or `lawn` under blades; snow with sparkles; speckled mycelium) creeping over faces that look up enough (ragged by noise) and hanging 2-6 texels down each side from the block's top edge. Leaves are `leafy`: leaf blobs from `pebbles` (stretched for spruce and palms), lit from the top left, holes where no leaf covers and the clump noise is thin (discarded in the fragment shader instead of the atlas alpha; shadows still use the atlas), closing up with distance; patterned from the position before the wind `sway`, so the leaves don't slide through the pattern. Cost is per shaded pixel, whatever the pattern's resolution. Below sea level, while the eye is underwater (`Elements::deep` in `weather.z`), the ground's albedo is absorbed by its depth below the eye's (`ABSORBED`, red first), emissive untouched.
- `submarine.rs` — submarines: replicated `Sub { at, yaw, pitch, hull, charge, modules, lit, thrust, rider }` (saved as `World::subs`). Right-click water holding a Submarine to `Submerge` it, right-click it to `Hatch` in, left-click an empty one to `Dock` it back (modules return to the inventory), right-click holding a module to `Fit` it (`BAYS` 5; Power Cells stack), iron ore patches the hull, fuel (`balloon::burns`) or an Aether Cell charges it, Shift+right-click `Strip`s the last module; aboard, R refits with the held item. The rider flies it client-side (`cruise`, `Craft::Submarine`): W/S along the view, A/D strafe, Space/C rise and dive, floats at the surface and falls out of water, L floodlight, F torpedo, Shift leaves; sends `Dive`. Modules: Sonar (64 m) / Deep Resonance Sonar (160 m, ores, precursor signals to 600 m): `ping` every `PING_EVERY` fills `Sweep` and `scope` draws a rotating radar (terrain slice at the sub's depth, creatures, hostiles, organisms, ores, ruins) with nearest signals listed; Drill Arm / Aether Lance (`drill`: speed, reach, 3x3 bore, used by `player::work` and validated in `authority::dig`; without a Sample Claw the drill shreds organisms, with one it harvests double); Floodlight (spot light); Thruster / Hydrojet (speed); Hull Plating / Resonant Plating (`rated` depth: 26 m, 50 m, unlimited; deeper `strain`s the hull, which wrecks at 0 and drops its modules); Torpedo Bay (`Fire`: a `Torpedo` bolt that blasts creatures); Power Cell (capacity). `charge` drains while ridden (more when thrusting or lit), a flat battery crawls at a quarter speed. Model: a hollow hull (`KEEL` to `MIDDLE` + `RADIUS`, the collision `BULK` lifted by `KEEL`): the outside is one `sculpt`ed rigid mesh (`hull`: a 12-sided tube open at the nose with steel seam bands (aether-cyan with resonant plating), a dark red belly, brass-rimmed portholes, a tapered stern, conning tower with periscope, hatch and dive planes, swept fins, keel, and each module's `fitting`), rebuilt when the modules change, plus the sculpted brass `screw`; its glass nose `BUBBLE` holds the pilot's eye, so first person sees the real interior (`hollow`: lined cabin, ribs, deck, seat, console with sonar screen and dials, control grips, ceiling pipes, lockers, bulkhead hatch, a cabin light) through the window frame; the outside and module parts hang under a `Fittings` node rebuilt when the modules change; the arm is hidden aboard; the hull tilts by a fifth of the view pitch about the pilot's eye, so the cabin stays put around the camera. Creatures attacking a rider hit the hull instead (`shield`). Breath: players have `LUNGS` seconds (times the swim stat) under water outside a sub (`breathe`, replicated `Air`, shown bottom right), then lose 2 health a second; creative never drowns. The `dive` opt seats a creative player in a fully fitted sub at `inside`.
- `mech.rs` — mechs (SS13 exosuit-like walkers): replicated `Mech { at, yaw, hull, charge, modules, lit, jets, striding, rider }` (saved as `World::mechs`). Bigger than a player (`BULK` 1.7 wide, 2.95 tall: needs a 2-wide, 3-tall gap); the pilot sits `SEAT` above the feet, so `Pilot::at` is the cockpit and the eye looks out through the glass canopy. Right-click a block holding a Mech to `Deploy` it (needs room), right-click it to `Board`, left-click an empty one to `Pack` it back (modules return), right-click holding an upgrade to `Refit` (`HARDPOINTS` 6; it also takes the sub's Floodlight, Hull/Resonant Plating and Power Cells), Shift+right-click `Unfit`s the last; iron ore patches the frame, fuel or an Aether Cell charges it; aboard, R refits. The pilot walks it client-side (`walk`, `Craft::Mech`): WASD walk (slower wading, never floats: walks the sea floor), steps up one block on its own, Space hops (or fires `Jumpjets`), F laser, X missile (uses torpedoes), L light, Shift leaves (`alight` beside it or on top); sends `Stride`. Upgrades (`Block::rig`): Hydraulic Clamp (punches `CRUSHING` times harder, `folk::strike`), Mech Drill / Diamond Mech Drill (`drill`, like the sub's), Ore Box (dug drops go straight into the inventory, `authority::dig`), Jump Jets, Mech Laser and Missile Rack (`Shoot`: server `Shot`s riding a `spell::Bolt`, `volley`), Mech Sleeper (heals the pilot), Repair Droid (mends the frame, orbiting drone), Heat Shielding (walks through lava unharmed), Build Arm (`BUILD_REACH` for placing, checked in `authority::put`), Dynamo (recharges). Hazards (`endure`): the cabin is sealed while charged (`sealed`): no drowning (`submarine::breathe`), no lava burns (`condition::scorch`), creature blows hit the frame (`shield`, scaled by `armour`); pressure past `rated` depth (40 m, 80 m plated, any with resonant plating) and lava without heat shielding wear the frame; at 0 it wrecks and drops its modules. Unridden mechs fall. Client: `assemble` builds a cockpit (glass canopy, hidden from its own pilot in first person, console, grips, light, floodlight `Beam`) under a bobbing `Torso`; the outside is `sculpt`ed like the creatures (rigid vertex-coloured meshes over one white material, `Coat` by plating: orange paint, grey plating, scorched): `frame` (rebuilt when modules change) is the tapered hull, roof, hazard-striped chin, hips, side plates, vents, shoulders, both arms with their `tools` (spiral drill bit, clamp claws, laser barrel or fist) and each module's `gear`; `outfit` adds the glowing bits (lamps, jet `Flame`s, missile tips, power cells, the repair `Drone`); `strut`/`pose` walk the two IK legs (`knee`; rigid `thigh`, `shin` with knee cap and greave, `foot` with toes) from the measured motion and play `Cue::Stomp` on footfalls. The `mech` opt seats the player in a fully fitted mech. `cargo test --features dev mechs -- --ignored` writes `screenshots/mechs.ppm` and `submarines` writes `screenshots/submarines.ppm` (four views, shaded and wireframe).
- `precursor.rs` — sunken precursor bases (the word "precursor" is code-only: in game the builders are never named, only implied: Sunken Alloy, Sunken Glass, Sentinel, "Unknown signal"), planned as `dungeon::Dungeon`s of `Style::Precursor` (`Dungeon::within` scans every cell, island or not) on flat deep sea floor (`CHANCE` of open cells, below `SHALLOWEST`, never near home): a flooded alloy dome hall (`Stamp::Flood` fills with water) with glass bands, an aether crown and conduit pillars, a sealed air-filled glass `sanctum` with a cache in the middle (dig in and the sea floods it), alloy tubes to pods (`Pod::Garden` with bio lab and living specimens, `Vault` and, deeper, `Reactor`), slight decay, aether lamps as lights; caches use `dungeon::loot` tier `TIER` (alloy scrap, aether cells, resonant crystals, datacores, pearls, torpedoes). Lairs of `Foe::Sentinel` and (reactor) `Foe::Custodian`.
- `construct.rs` — precursor constructs (folk `Band::Sentinel`, `Band::Custodian`, sculpted, skinned to the folk joints with their own `pivot`s): the Sentinel is a floating armoured orb with a glowing eye, a vein ring, swaying vanes and tendrils; it flies and hurls frost bolts (`Foe::hurls`); the Custodian is a hulking alloy golem (`CUSTODIAN_SCALE`) with visor and chest core that walks the sea floor (`Foe::heavy`: doesn't float) and weakens what it hits. `cargo test --features dev constructs -- --ignored` writes `screenshots/constructs.ppm`.
- `biology.rs` — `grow` (authority, every `GROW_EVERY`): random organisms near players spread over the sea floor (not when `crowded`), kelp climbs toward the surface and glowkelp grows up to three tall on land. The Bio Lab (`recipe::DEPTHS`, `Cost::Free` at `Block::BioLab`) cultures every species with a nutrient, turns clams into pearls, grows reefstone, nacre and biolume, splices glowkelp, and offers bio routes to the floodlight, sonar and power cell. Goals chapter `Depths` catalogues the species and the submarine and precursor milestones.
- `gear.rs` — tools and equipment: `Material` (Wood, the rocks, Basalt, Copper, Tin, Iron, Gold, Diamond, Lumen and Glimsteel, Verdantine, Hexbrass, Mithril (from its infused ingot) and the dungeon-only Ancient Steel) with a `Grit { hardness, toughness }`, its `ingredient` block (`of`), `smelted` (ores: forged at the furnace with coal), `palette`, `speed` (digging multiplier from hardness) and `edge` (sword damage); a tool is craftable from any material that `suits` it (`needs`: pickaxe hardness 4 toughness 2, axe 2/2, shovel 1/2, sword 2/3; `heads`: head and handle counts); `dug_with` names the tool a block wants (shovel: soils, axe: wood, sword: cobwebs, pickaxe: other solids). `Wear` (Head, Neck, Body, Waist, Feet, Finger: the `WORN` equipment slots, `of(block)`). `Gear { material, perks }` rides on a `protocol::Stack`; `Perk { virtue, by }` with `Virtue`s (Swift, Springy, Swimmer, Fireproof, Frostward, Antidote, Warding, Might, Delving, Fortune, Quickcast, Thrift, Vigor, Mending: `line`, `title` ("of Haste")); `title`/`lines` describe a stack; `worn(inventory, hand)` are the perks of the worn gear and the held tool, folded into `skill::Stats` by `Stats::geared` / `Skills::geared`; `found(seed, salt, tier)` rolls a dungeon find (a tool of a better material or a wearable, 1-3 subtle perks from the item's `pool`, stronger at tier 2): chests give one per tier, the Bone Warden drops one.
- `machine.rs` — fluids, pipes and power (authority simulation, shared rules): `Liquid` (Water, Lava, Crude, Diesel, Biofuel, and `arcana.rs`'s Mana and Blood; mB, `BUCKET` = 1000); `design(block)` gives each machine's `Design` (`intake` liquids and room, `outlet` room, `draws`/`makes` W, `stores` J, `hopper` for fed items); `bore` is a pipe tier's capacity and rate; `plumbed`/`wired` say what pipes and cables `joins`. Server `Works` (`states`: `State { block, intake, outlet, stock, charge }` per machine cell, saved as `World::works`, `settle` at load; `install` on `Put` and admin `set`) runs every `TICK` (0.25 s) in `run`: drops states whose block is gone, then `power` (cables and electric machines touching form grids: generators offer power when fuelled (steam: fed fuel seconds + piped water; biofuel; diesel; geothermal: a lava source beside it or piped lava; solar: open sky by day times `Sky::sun`; wind: height above `SEA` times `Sky::wind`), consumers want it when they have work (electric pump beside a water or lava source, pumpjack standing on an `OilSeep`, refinery with crude, electric furnace charging, lamps); generators throttle to demand plus battery room, batteries cover deficits), `labour` (burn fuel, pump, refine crude into diesel, ferment `biomass` items into biofuel, charge furnaces; lamps swap to `ElectricLampLit` with an `Altered`), then `plumb` (each connected group of pipes and tanks pools one liquid (the most plentiful wins), filling pipes first and sharing the rest among tanks; machine outlets push into and intakes pull from touching pools, limited by the slowest pipe's rate; outlets also feed touching intakes directly). Each cell's state is replicated as a `Unit` entity (`at`, liquids (pipes quantised to eighths, tanks to 48ths), stock, charge, power, its `Grid`, `Status` with a `line`). Drains (`FluidDrain`, `ItemDrain`) sit against a container (`attached`: the first `container` neighbour, above first): `drain` takes `DRAINED` mB/s out of a tank or machine (its outlet first), spilt (clients show `fx::spill` particles in the liquid's colour at the model's `spout`) or, once a copper pipe is fitted (`Tend`, making a `PipedDrain`, which drops the pipe again when dug), pushed into the pipes around it (never back into its own container); `empty` takes one item a second from a chest's coffer: into a free `ItemPipe` beside it (it waits while they are all busy), dropped straight down onto a conveyor below, or thrown out as a `Loose` item. Item pipes (`ItemPipe`, `carries`: pipes, chests, item drains, steam engines, fermenters, belts) hold one `Parcel { stack, from, to, progress }` each (in `State::parcel`, replicated on the `Unit`): `ferry` moves it `PARCEL_SPEED` cells/s through the pipe and hands it on at the exit (`deliver`: into the next empty pipe, a chest's coffer (`takes`: room; an unopened loot chest refuses), a steam engine's or fermenter's hopper, or `onto_belt` as a `Loose` item); a pipe that refuses turns it round; `route` picks the exit (not back the way it came, unless it must): a neighbour that `takes` the item first, then other pipes and belts, rotating by the pipe's counter in `stock`; a dug pipe drops its parcel. Client message `Tend { at, slot }` (right-click a machine holding something it `accepts`): fuel into a steam engine, biomass into a fermenter, a water or lava bucket into a tank or matching intake, an empty bucket out of a tank or outlet. `Works::charged`/`smelt`: a charged electric furnace counts as a furnace station without coal (`recipe::Recipe::electric`), each craft spending `SMELT` J. `cargo test machine` runs a diesel line and a pumpjack.
- `arcana.rs` — magic (authority simulation over `machine::Works`: arcane blocks are `machine()`s, their `State` carries `item` (the stack set on a holder), `essence` (one count per `Aspect`), `done` (a finished product waiting to be taken), `status` and `link`, all shown on the `Unit`). Mana (`machine::Liquid::Mana`) comes from generating flowers (`Block::bloomer`, modelled, crafted at the `BloomFont` station from the `glimmer` flowers that `generate::bloom` scatters on grassy islands; bone dust doubles them): `Sunsip` (open sky by day), `Dewcup` (beside a water source), `Emberbell` (burns fuel: `Tend` or `Loose` fuel tossed within `EATS_WITHIN`); each `bind`s its mana to the nearest `ManaCistern` within `BIND`. Pools and the `Cauldron` are `vessel`s (modelled, not solid, so tossed `Loose` items fall in): a pool `soak`s pool rites (iron ore to Glimsteel, diamond ore to Starheart, lumen to Tidepearl, bone dust to arcane dust); a crucible holding water over something `hot` `steep`s items into essence (`aspects`) or, for a catalyst whose essence is there, brews it (Hexbrass, Lumen, saplings, coal, feathers) and throws the product out. `CrimsonAltar` (`holder`): right-click with the `RitualKnife` costs `SACRIFICE` health for lifeblood (`Liquid::Lifeblood`), creatures dying within `SLAIN_REACH` add more (`sacrificed`), `CrimsonRune`s in the 5x2x5 around and below raise its room and speed (count kept in `stock`); an item set on it `bleed`s into its rite's product (stone to Bare Tablet, Bound Tablet, diamond ore to Heartstone). `ConfluenceAltar` (`kindle`): a catalyst on it plus items on `Plinth`s within 3 blocks matching an infusion rite (Verdantine, Mithril Ingot) or a `Charm` (a tool or wearable gains a `gear::Perk`) draws `INFUSE_RATE` mana from pools within `DRAWN_FROM`. Holders: right-click with an item sets one on it, right-click again takes it back (Shift+right-click opens the panel). Rites are `recipe::Recipe`s whose station is the arcane block and whose `Cost` is `Mana`, `Blood` or `Essence`: never crafted by hand, but shown in the recipe browser with `Recipe::how` and in each block's panel (`apparatus::lexicon`). The `WellspringSigil` pours water without being used up. New tool materials: Glimsteel, Verdantine, Hexbrass, and Mithril from its ingot. Client (`apparatus.rs`): liquid levels in pools, crucibles and altars (`basin`), held items hovering over holders (`display`), glowing `Mote`s from flowers to their pool and from pedestals to the altar (`sparkle`). `cargo test arcana` runs a pool, an infusion, a charm and a blood altar.

- `texture.rs` — 16 px pixel-art tiles, hand-drawn: `Art` (16 strings of palette shades `0`–`3`, `.` = see through) coloured by a `Palette`, several tiles share one drawing (`drawn`, `solid`, `cutout`, `ore` spots over stone); grass and mycelium tops are `turf` (lattice clouds with staggered blade strokes), `fringed` sides hang ragged turf (2–6 px per column, shadowed edge) over `soil` (dirt, flecked like stone); buckets are one drawing whose inner rim row takes the fluid's colour; planks, bricks, glass, lamp, basalt, waystones are geometric; the rock varieties are the stone and cobblestone drawings in `shades` of each `rock` colour. - `mesh.rs` — `lone(block, at, seed, torches)` meshes one block alone as triangles of `Corner`s (the crumble shell). `Padded::gather` copies a chunk plus a 1-block border from its 26 neighbours, `build` emits culled faces (no greedy meshing: the atlas can't repeat) as plain cubes by default; with the `rounded` opt (`rounds`) every `Look::Opaque` block keeps its cube, its outer edges arced and inner edges filleted with radius `ROUND` (in `UNIT` fixed-point steps per block); faces are subdivided at one stop `ROUND` in from each corner only where `bent_at` (a position-only test, so neighbours never crack), each vertex placed by `rounded` (the sum of the bends of the lattice edges around it, from each edge's 2×2 `Section` of rounding cells) with a blended normal; ambient occlusion and daylight are blended bilinearly across the subdivided face; fluids, leaves, glass, logs and models are not rounded, collisions (`hull`) stay cubic; models are meshed from `model::shape(block, links)` (pipes and cables by their neighbours); `projection` blocks (soft natural ones: stone, cobbles, sand, dirt, gravel, ores, and the `turf` blocks grass, snow and mycelium) carry their top tile's `texture::tile_origin` as UV and `-1 - (flank * 256 + bottom)` tile indices in `UV_1.y`, and `ground.wgsl` paints them by world-space triplanar projection (`textureSampleGrad`, sharp blend), so textures don't follow block faces or stretch on smoothed shapes; turf is its dirt with the top tile creeping over every face that faces up enough (ragged by noise) (`lone` meshes for the ghost keep per-face UVs); `UV_1.x` is `2 * owner + leafy`, the owning cell's index in the chunk (-2 for none), which the shaders decode to discard the cell being dug and make the aimed cell glow; quads take the diagonal that doesn't fold; per-vertex ambient occlusion (quad flipped against anisotropy) times a sky term (depth below the generated surface, so caves go dark), split into `solid` (opaque, single-sided: `Look::Opaque` cubes and lava, so the GPU keeps early depth tests), `cutout` (alpha-masked, double-sided: leaves, glass, cobwebs, logs, models), `liquid` and `unshadowed` (torches, spawned with `NotShadowCaster`) meshes, plus the chunk's `hull::Hull` (every solid cell's faces toward non-solid cells, at the contoured corners; solid models as cubes); fluid tops are lowered by level (`crest`; full height under the same fluid), sides step down against lower levels, lava goes into the solid (emissive) mesh; `UV_1.y` is otherwise a plant's sway height in its cell; the vertex colour's alpha carries the sky term (the rgb is already darkened by it), so `ground.wgsl` can undo it for block light (`shine.rs`); logs are smooth-shaded cylinders (`trunk`: `TRUNK_SIDES` sides of `TRUNK_RADIUS`, the bark tile once per quarter, outside the contouring; they don't hide neighbours' faces; sides facing an opaque neighbour are hidden, tops and bottoms against logs too; `Builder::polygon` fans convex faces); water is opaque and glossy, its vertex colour tinted by the depth to the generated floor (`SHALLOWS` turquoise to `OPEN_SEA`).
- `lawn.rs` + `sward.wgsl` + `thicket.wgsl` + `meadow.wgsl` — grass as hundreds of fine blades per block, in a Genshin-like style. A `SPAN`² `Rgba32Float` grass map addressed by world position modulo `SPAN` (`tops`: per column the topmost grass surface height, its world x and z to tell stale texels, and whether the blades grow tall there: `swarded`, noisy swards by moisture, no blocks involved) is filled by `survey` tasks per chunk column within `AROUND` of the pilot (canopy leaves, branches and plants are looked through; grass under a trunk is not grass), resurveyed when `stream.rs` remeshes a chunk (`Lawns::stale`, `outdated` when it changed mid-survey; `Progress.lawns`). `meadow.wgsl` (shader library `bloxy::meadow`) defines the one blade field both layers draw: `CELL` (1/16 block) cells of `PER_CELL` blades, each `blade` (root jittered in its cell, height from soft `lush` patches and `tallness` (each shader blends the tall-grass flags of the four nearest blocks at a noise-warped point, `spread`, so tall patches have no block edges), straight `axis` leaning by the smooth `field`: drift noise, rolling `gust`s from `Elements::wind`, trampling away from the pilot's `tread`; width, facing, a `chance` that hands it between layers). Near the pilot (`NEAR`, crossfaded over `HANDOVER` by `handed`) `sward.wgsl` draws them as geometry: the `tufts` mesh (`BLADES_PER_BLOCK` slots per block in a `CLOSE` square that follows the pilot) curves each blade from root to the same tip. Beyond, out to `OUT` (`thinned`), `thicket.wgsl` ray-marches them: `volumes` is a grid of boxes (a top and four sides per block, hidden where no grass or a neighbour covers the side) around each grass column from just below the ground to `BAND` above it; the fragment shader walks the eye ray through the blade cells (sheared by the entry point's lean so leaning blades are found where their tips are, and through block boundaries), intersects each cell's blades as tapered planes (widened to at least a pixel), shades the nearest with PBR lighting and writes its depth, or discards so the ground shows. Both use a `StandardMaterial` with diffuse transmission (backlit glow), not shadow-casting; blades over the dug cell vanish. `lawn` is the ground colour that replaces the grass top tile in `ground.wgsl` (`Surface::turf`): dark like blade roots under the blades, the blades' mid colour with gust sheen beyond them. The blades are optional (`Settings::blades`, the Grass setting, off by default): without them `mow` hides both layers, surveys stop, and `turf.z` makes the ground draw `fuzz` instead, a cheap fuzz with gust sheen (`strands`: streaky blade fibres along three directions blended by noise, gentle contrast), fading to the mid colour with distance.
- `hull.rs` — collision with the smoothed surface (client): `Hulls` (chunk key → `Hull`: cell → `Facet { normal, corners }`, from the meshing tasks); `push` (a sphere out of the nearby triangles), `floor` (the highest up-facing surface under a vertical segment), `cast` (nearest front-facing triangle along a ray: its cell and face normal), `entry` (ray vs box).

- `cage.rs` — box modelling with Catmull-Clark subdivision (ported from Northmark): a `Cage` is points + polygon faces + edge `creases`. The clouds sort behind every other transparent (`Cloud::depth_bias`) so plates show in front of them. Player name tags (`figure.rs`, in the player's hue) and creature readouts use it.
- `chat.rs` — text chat: Enter or T opens a line (`Menu::chatting`, which `menu::closed`/`Menu::idle` count as busy so movement and shortcuts pause), Enter sends `Say`, Esc cancels; heard `Said` lines (name in its hue) stack at the bottom left, the last `RECENT` shown for `SHOWN_FOR` s, all `KEPT` while typing.
- `hud.rs` — `SHADE` is the hard 1 px black text shadow; `scale` sets `UiScale` from the window height (720 logical px = 1, quarter steps, never below 1) so every UI element grows with the screen; crosshair, `Notice` line above the hotbar (fades after `ANNOUNCED_FOR`), the held item's name (`gear::title`) above it for `NAMED_FOR` s after switching slots (`name_held`), hotbar (the `Belt`: one gray `STRIP` without padding, so the selected outline reaches its edges, `SLOT` 46 px with 40 px icons from `Palette.icons`, the selected slot outlined in `PICKED`, `BELT_BOTTOM` above the screen's bottom; it stays in place above the menu's dimming on the Inventory and Chest tabs, where its slots are the inventory's hotbar buttons (`Act::Slot`), and the pages leave `BELT_ROOM` for it under the centred backpack) and counts (`Cooling` shades a spell's slot while it recharges), the experience bar above it (`Learning`, the level beside it, `+` while points wait), status line (role/connection, players, position, target, fps, chunks loading), health hearts (9 px drawings, each texel a whole number of screen pixels near 2 UI px whatever the `UiScale`, as many as the player's maximum health allows, wrapping upward) above the hotbar and a red flash when hurt, block outline only for signs (12 edge bars around `model::bounds`; every other target glows through `ground::Aimed`). `icon_rect(block)` is the rect of a block's icon in the icon atlas.
- `shine.rs` — block light as a 3D texture instead of point lights: `Shine` is an `Rgba8Unorm` `SPAN` (128 x 64 x 128) volume, one texel per block, centred on the eye in `STEP`s; `relight` rebuilds it in a task (`kindle`) when the eye moves a step or a chunk within reach of it is inserted or edited (`Voxels::relit`), then hands it and its corner (`Surface::shone`) to every `Ground`. Every `radiance` block (torches, braziers, lumen, lamps, biolume, glowkelp: a colour and a reach) floods its light through open cells (`spread`: Dial's algorithm over 26 neighbours with costs 1, sqrt 2, sqrt 3, no squeezing past opaque corners, so walls hold it back and it bends round corners), falling off as `(1 - x)^2 / (1 + 20 x^2)` with `x = d / reach`, added up per channel and stored softly capped (`1 - exp(-light / BRIGHTEST)`, square-root encoded). `ground.wgsl` (`shined`) samples it trilinearly half a block out along the normal and adds the albedo (without the sky darkening) times it after PBR lighting, so it reads the same by day and night; it fades out at the volume's edge. Only chunk meshes get it (creatures and water don't); moving lights (held torches, spells) are still point lights. `cargo test shine` checks a wall; `cargo test --features dev kindling -- --ignored --nocapture` times a rebuild around the home dungeon.
- `sky.rs` — physical atmosphere (`atmosphere()`, left out in plain mode, see Build) (aerial perspective reaches only `UNHAZED` metres, so it doesn't haze the land: clouds are drawn after it and would not match); `daylight(hour)` is the light for the fixed `hour`: sun (a large `SUN_DISK`) or, at night, the moon (`MOONLIGHT`, blue), exposure by sun elevation (`DAY_EXPOSURE` 13.6 down to `NIGHT_EXPOSURE` 7.2), haze from day through dusk to night; cascaded shadows, a bluish ambient that rises at night, `lens()` (exposure, ACES, bloom, a `DistanceFog` that is empty until rain or water sets it); `torchlight()` is the torch point light (also on held torches). `kindle` gives the `TORCH_EMBERS` nearest burning torches and braziers an ember emitter (at `model::flame`; `fx::Fire::TORCH` embers, the larger `Fire::BRAZIER` blaze); block light itself is `shine.rs`.

# Opts
Natively the env var `BLOXY` holds JSON5 `opts::Opts`; in the browser the URL query (`?connect=wss://host:port&name=Ann`, or `?opts={…}` for anything else). `connect` defaults to the build-time env var `BLOXY_CONNECT` (the Pages build sets the hosted server); `?connect=` (empty) plays solo. Fields: `connect` (server URL: guest), `serve: port` (headless dedicated server), `host: port` (play and serve), `name`, `password`, `menu: 'inventory'|'skills'|'goals'|'settings'|'profile'|'skin'|'waystones'|'sign'|'map'` (start with the menu open; screenshots then don't wait for a player), `save` (world file; dedicated servers default to `world.json` in the working directory, other roles don't save unless given), `seed` (1), `reach` (view radius in chunks; 9 native, 6 web), `hour` (10), `creative` (starter hotbar and backpack with stacks of building blocks, torches, stations, a boat and buckets, spell fuels, a few spells and a brazier; new accounts know the whole skill tree; fast digging), `shot: <secs>` (screenshot to `screenshots/shot-$SHOT_NAME.png` once loaded, then exit), `at: [dx, dz]` (spawn offset from the spawn point, on the ground), `inside: [x, y, z]` (spawn at that point, e.g. inside a dungeon), `dive` (with `creative`: start in a fully fitted submarine), `mech` (start in a fully fitted mech), `rounded` (round off block edges, see `mesh.rs`; off by default: blocks are cubes), `yaw`, `pitch` (degrees), `press: [[secs, 'LMB'|'RMB'|'W'|'A'|'S'|'D'|'Space'|'Ctrl'|'Shift'|'Tab'|'Esc'|'E'|'Q'|'V'|'1'…'9'|'0'], …]` (taps of 0.15 s; a tap shorter than a frame is released the next frame), `hold: [[from, to, key], …]`, `trader: secs` (first trader ship), `raid: [secs, 'pirates'|'vikings']` (first raid), `weather: 'clear'|'cloudy'|'rain'|'storm'` (fixed weather), `admin: port` (admin command port; 7778 by default when serving or hosting).
e.g. `BLOXY='{shot: 2, yaw: 40, pitch: -15}' SHOT_NAME=valley tools/shot`. Screenshots are the way to check visuals — always look at them.

# Multiplayer
- Dedicated server: `BLOXY='{serve: 7777}' cargo run --features dev`. Browser pages served over https (GitHub Pages) can only open `wss://`, so put the server behind a TLS proxy (e.g. Caddy `reverse_proxy localhost:7777`) and join with `?connect=wss://your.host`. Native clients use `ws://host:7777`.
- Hosting: `.github/workflows/server.yml` builds the server on GitHub Actions (`--profile server`: release without LTO, stripped) on pushes to `master` or the working branch, verifies the host key fingerprint, uploads the binary to `/opt/bloxy/bin/bloxy` as the `bloxy` user (secret `DEPLOY_KEY`) and waits for the `bloxy-restart.path` unit to restart the service. `deploy/setup.sh` is the one-time root bootstrap of the Debian host: ufw (22, 80, 443, 7777), the `bloxy` user and its deploy key (printed for the secret), the `bloxy` service (`serve: 7777`, working dir `/opt/bloxy/world`) and Caddy terminating TLS for `wss://$DOMAIN`. Cloud sessions can't SSH out.
- `tools/duo` runs a dedicated server and two clients under xvfb and screenshots the second seeing the first: the quickest end-to-end check of netcode.
- Movement is client-authoritative; blocks, inventories and player list are server-authoritative.
- The hosted world lives in `/opt/bloxy/world/world.json` (edits, accounts, claims). When generation changes, bump `generate::GENERATION`: the new server keeps claimed chunks from their saved snapshots and drops other edits. `bloxy admin regen [seed]` on the host does the same on demand; deleting the file (service stopped) starts from nothing.
- Admin over SSH: `/opt/bloxy/bin/bloxy admin help` talks to the running service on 127.0.0.1:7778 (look at the world as text maps or PNGs, place blocks, spawn and remove mobs, regenerate). The browser's solo mode doesn't save.
- The UI font has no `−`, `×`, `°` or `…` glyphs: use ASCII.

# Build
Priority is fast incremental builds over runtime speed. Debug builds only; release only when asked.
- `cargo check` to verify compilation; `cargo run --features dev` to play (`dev` = Bevy dynamic linking).
- `tools/shot <name> '<opts>'` builds and screenshots under xvfb.
- Cloud sessions have no GPU: wrap runs in `xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1600x900x24"` (lavapipe, software Vulkan, slow frames).
- Bevy runs without default features: `Cargo.toml` lists only what the game uses. Add a feature there when using a new Bevy part.
- Web build: `trunk build --release` (`index.html`, feature `webgpu`), deployed to GitHub Pages (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`, Settings → Pages → Source: GitHub Actions) on pushes to `master` or the working branch, joining the hosted server by default. Browsers without a WebGPU adapter are sent to `plain/`: `trunk build plain.html --release --dist dist/plain` (feature `webgl2`, which turns on `plain`). Plain mode (`sky::PLAIN`) has no compute shaders: no `bevy_hanabi` particles (`fx::Sparkle` only registers `EffectAsset`, effects spawn but never draw) and no physical atmosphere (a flat `ClearColor` sky, ambient light scaled by exposure instead of the atmosphere's environment light); it says so on the loading screen, once in the log and in the status line. `cargo run --features dev,plain` previews it natively. Both pages inline `web/errors.js` (before the game loads): a DOM box at the bottom left that collects uncaught errors, failed promises and `console.error` (Bevy's panic hook and error logs land there), collapses repeats (the `closure invoked ... after being dropped` flood that follows a panic), marks the first one, and lists the browser, WebGPU and WebGL2 renderer (flagging software rendering) and limits, with Copy and Close; the game's own `console.rs` log is drawn by the game and dies with it. New visuals must work in plain mode or be skipped there.
- On a panic or startup failure (including Bevy system param conflicts), rerun with `RUST_BACKTRACE=1`.
- Docs: https://docs.rs/bevy/0.19.1/bevy/ , https://docs.rs/bevy_replicon/0.44.3/bevy_replicon/

# Style
Code structure should mirror the thinking behind it: translate the user's conceptual framework fairly directly into code. Functional-ish.
- Format with `cargo +nightly fmt` (repo `rustfmt.toml` uses nightly-only options). Avoid comments; name things well.
- Order within a file: if A refers to B, A comes after B. Keep related code together.
- Semicolons are a verbosity signal: prefer expressions over statements.
- No `return`, `continue`, `let … else`, or early exits.
- Prefer let chains over nested `if let`/`if` or `.and_then()`.
- Prefer `Option` methods over matching on `Option` (not so for most other enums).
- Prefer `find`, `find_map`, `any`, `fold` over loops with break (socket read loops are the exception).
- Use `for` loops for side effects, never `for_each`.
- Eagerly destructure: `let &Thing { field } = thing()`.
- Domain model with named instances, newtypes and associated consts.
- No more function-calls-function indirection than needed.
- ECS: merge components that always co-occur. Name components by behaviour, not entity.
